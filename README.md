## Business context

As IT/Enterprise architects, one of our primary modes of communication is through the use of architectural diagrams. These visual representations serve as critical artifacts that we can share with our customers and teams to ensure everyone is on the same page. The challenge with architecture is that we often need to operate at different levels of abstraction --- from the strategic to the tactical and physical. Architectural diagrams allow us to effectively communicate our ideas at every level.

Understanding how to leverage diagrams and create them is an essential skill for any aspiring architect. In this post, I'll walk you through the power of architectural diagrams and provide step-by-step guidance on how you can start creating your own.

Architectural diagrams use lines, arrows, and labeled components to illustrate the flow of data, processes, and interactions within a system. The goal is to create a clear, easy-to-understand visual that facilitates seamless communication.
